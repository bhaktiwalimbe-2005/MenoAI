# MenoAI
A One Stop AI based MENSTRUAL  tracker which tracks the Cycle, Symptoms and also the Severity of the symptoms . 
It also includes an AI chatbot for Medical Guidance . 
The AI-Based Menstrual Tracker is an intelligent and comprehensive digital health solution designed to assist women in monitoring and managing their menstrual health effectively. This one-stop platform leverages the power of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to track menstrual cycles, predict upcoming periods, and analyze the severity and patterns of symptoms such as cramps, mood changes, fatigue, and other health indicators.
